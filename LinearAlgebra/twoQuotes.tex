\begin{quoted}{\cite{Arianrhod2003} [p.96] in discussing
Maxwell's pioneering work on electromagnetism}
\ldots\ although precision and accuracy are vital in the
end, real mathematical skill is linguistic and imaginative
rather than arithmetical: mathematics is a language for
thinking dramatic new thoughts, not merely for doing
accurate book-keeping.
\end{quoted}

\begin{quoted}{J.~C. Maxwell in a letter to Tait 
\cite[p.224]{Arianrhod2003}}
The virtue of vectors lies not so much in \emph{solving} 
hard questions, as in enabling us to see the \emph{meaning} 
of the question and of its solution.
\end{quoted}
